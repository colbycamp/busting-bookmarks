      How it works & privacy

      Search uses your browser’s bookmark titles, addresses and full folder paths. It ignores case and accents, and every search word must match. With no search words, Best match shows newest bookmarks first. It does not search article contents.

      Save creates a native bookmark in the folder you choose. Existing bookmarks, order, folders and metadata are left in place. Matching addresses are shown before saving another copy; query parameters and fragments remain significant.

      Your browser remains the source of truth. Use its bookmark manager for HTML export/import and folder management. The extension remembers only the last folder you saved into, on this device.

      No extension uploads, page extraction, analytics or accounts. Your browser’s own bookmark synchronization settings still apply. Opening a result visits its website normally. Non-web bookmarks remain searchable but cannot be opened here.

      For address-bar search, type bb, press Tab or Space, then search. To assign a toolbar shortcut, use your browser’s extension shortcut settings.
